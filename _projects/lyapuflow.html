LyapuFlow:Controlling Generative Flows with
Lyapunov Feedback for Inverse Problems

Minseon Gwak1,4, Hans Hao-Hsun Hsu2, Danielle C. Maddix3, N. Benjamin Erichson1,4

1 Lawrence Berkeley National Lab · 2 Georgia Institute of Technology · 3 Siemens · 4 International Computer Science Institute

Paper arXiv BibTeX

Abstract

Pretrained flow models are now widely used as generative priors in science and vision, where inference-time guidance enables test-time constraints without retraining. Existing methods use projection, posterior sampling, or iterative optimization of the generative trajectory. We propose LyapuFlow, an alternative based on Lyapunov feedback control. At each sampling step, LyapuFlow predicts the terminal sample towards which the current flow is evolving, and evaluates the constraint violation on this prediction. Then, we compute the minimum-norm control that satisfies a prescribed Lyapunov decrease condition. The resulting control remains inactive when the uncontrolled dynamics already reduce the constraint violation at the prescribed rate. Otherwise, it provides a corrective update within a feedback trust region that prevents the control from dominating the pretrained dynamics. We demonstrate LyapuFlow in both data and latent spaces, outperforming alternatives spanning different mechanisms for test-time constraint enforcement in scientific machine learning and image inverse problems.

Method Overview

LyapuFlow adds inference-time feedback control to a pretrained flow model. It evaluates constraints on a predicted final sample, then chooses a minimum-norm correction using a Lyapunov decrease condition. A trust region limits the correction relative to the learned flow. No retraining is required.

Results · Data space

Scientific inverse problems

LyapuFlow guides PDE solution generation for 1D heat, Burgers, and reaction–diffusion (RD) equations and 2D Navier–Stokes (NS), enforcing linear and nonlinear constraints such as initial conditions, local dynamics, and conservation laws.

Task adaptability through decay-rate tuning. Unlike other samplers, which require task-specific tuning of the guidance step size, LyapuFlow can adapt relatively easily across tasks and models by adjusting its Lyapunov decay rate.

Higher sample fidelity while satisfying physical constraints. The top panel shows average solutions for a fixed initial-condition group, and the bottom panel shows pointwise absolute error to ground truth. Constraints include the initial condition, nonlinear local dynamics enforced with a Godunov update over the first five time steps, and mass balance.

Results · Latent space

Image inverse problems

We study image inverse problems in the established image latent space of pretrained flow-matching models such as Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium. A degraded image from AFHQ, FFHQ and DIV2K datasets defines a measurement constraint that is used to guide generation toward a clean reconstruction.

Reconstruction fidelity. The figure compares 4× super-resolution and motion-deblurring examples. Compared with FlowDPS, LyapuFlow avoids hallucinated antennae on the butterfly and better preserves high-frequency components in the cat reconstruction.

Sampling budget test. Gaussian deblurring examples compare FlowDPS and LyapuFlow at different number of function evaluations (NFEs). Although a larger budget reduces some FlowDPS artifacts, the freckles in the first row remain weakened and the eyelid crease in the second row stays absent; LyapuFlow preserves both details at either budget.
